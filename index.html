Spray tans

Golden, never orange.

Our airbrush tans use Lavish Tan, a brown-sugar solution derived from apples, so your color develops golden brown with no orange tones.

It’s organic, paraben-free and lightweight, with a naturally sweet scent and a flawless fade. Allure Magazine voted it the best organic airbrush tanning solution in the U.S.A. in 2012.

Since 2011

From 216 square feet to a pink palace.

In the spring of 2011, with only a hammer and a dream, Alli Parks transformed an itty-bitty office suite in Saint Helena into The Painted Lady. It held a desk, a Mystic Tan booth and a couple of free restaurant benches she found on the side of the road.

Today The Painted Lady thrives in its expansive, shimmering pink palace at 1643 Jefferson Street in Napa, with tanning experts, massage therapists, Reiki and an esthetician under one roof.

When she’s not painting the town bronze, Miss Parks enjoys champagne, lounging and instagramming her cat/soulmate, Francine.From Alli’s bio
