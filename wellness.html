01 · Red light therapy

The fountain of youth has arrived.

The Beauty Angel uses red light tuned to 633 nanometers to rejuvenate skin naturally. Skin cells called fibroblasts absorb the light and step up the body’s own production of collagen, elastin and hyaluronic acid. Small wrinkles soften, and skin looks visibly tauter and smoother.

It looks like a tanning booth but isn’t one: it emits no UV light. A professional-grade vibration platform tones, boosts circulation and makes the red light work even harder.

  • About three 12-minute sessions a week over the course of the regimen
  • After tanning: finish a UV session in the Beauty Angel to help your skin recover
  • Before a spray tan: it opens pores and speeds circulation for a more effective session
