What “high pressure” means

It refers to the type of lamp. Most of the UVB, or “burning,” rays are filtered out, so you’re exposed almost entirely to UVA, the rays that produce a golden-brown tan. It suits anyone who needs a tan quickly, or wants it to last longer than a tan from a traditional bed.

05 · Spray tan care

Before & after.

A little prep makes the glow last. More answers in the Glow Guide.

Before your tan

  1. Exfoliate and shave before your tan. The day before is best.
  2. Skip makeup, moisturizer, perfume and deodorant. They act as a barrier and stop the color from developing properly.
  3. Schedule workouts, waxing and manicure or pedicure appointments before your tan.
  4. Bring dark, loose-fitting clothing and sandals to wear afterward.

After your tan

  1. Don’t shower or get wet for 5–7 hours, or 2–4 hours for Lavish Express.
  2. After your first shower, moisturize with a paraben-free lotion at least twice a day for a week, for an even, flawless fade.
  3. Avoid chlorinated Jacuzzis, saunas and steam rooms, and limit intense workouts for a longer-lasting tan.
  4. Take cool showers instead of baths, and skip scrubs and scented body wash.
  5. A spray tan is not sunscreen. Apply an oil-free sunscreen before sun exposure.
